About LuxShop

We are a small online tech shop based in Brussels, passionate about bringing high-quality technology products to customers across Europe. Our goal is simple — to offer reliable products at honest prices while making online shopping easy and enjoyable for everyone. From keyboards and gaming mice to monitors and mobile accessories, every item in our store is carefully selected by our team.

LuxShop started in 2022 with a small collection of products and a big vision. Since then, we have continued to grow thanks to the trust and support of our customers. We believe shopping online should be fast, secure, and stress-free. That is why we focus on good customer service, quick delivery, and quality products that people can truly rely on every day.

Our mission is not only to sell tech products, but also to help people create better workspaces, gaming setups, and digital experiences. We are always searching for new and innovative products to add to our collection so our customers can stay up to date with the latest technology trends.
